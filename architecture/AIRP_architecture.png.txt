AIRP has 5 modules:
Module 1 - Phishing Analysis Engine (VirusTotal API)
Module 2 - Threat Intel Feed (AbuseIPDB + AlienVault OTX)
Module 3 - Automated IR Playbooks (3 scenarios, PDF output)
Module 4 - Vulnerability Reporting (OpenVAS input, Power BI)
Module 5 - Compliance Tracker (NIST CSF mapping, Excel output)

All modules feed into a unified reports/ output folder.
